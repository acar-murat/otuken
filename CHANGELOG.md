# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org): MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH. While the major version is 0 the game is in early development and anything may change between minor versions.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-24)

First playable version.

- 4v4 matches on the Ötüken plain: hold the Kut Tree until your side's claim reaches 100%.
- Eight heroes from Turkic mythology in two sides. Tengri's Host has Alp Er Tunga, Asena, Mergen and Umay; Erlik's Horde has Tepegöz, Albastı, Şulmus and Erlik. Each hero has a weapon, a skill and an ultimate.
- Bots fill every other seat; rival strength can be Easy, Normal or Hard.
- Touch controls (two sticks plus ability buttons), with keyboard and mouse in the editor.
- Android APK and an unsigned iPhone build for Sideloadly, built in the cloud.
